About the project

This project began when I first watched "Desperate Housewives", the wonderful series by Marc Cherry. Every episode was packed with idioms and natural expressions that I never encountered in textbooks. I started writing them down, first for myself, then realizing they could help other learners like me — people who want to master real, living English, not just the grammar from school or university materials. This collection is the result. It’s a growing phrasebook built from the shows we watch, the games we play, and the conversations we hear.

What you can learn:

  • Authentic English from the shows, games, and media you already love
  • Practical vocabulary for daily life, not just grammar rules
  • How to spot, collect, and remember useful phrases on your own
